    Statistics#

    The Statistics screen shows a time-series chart of historical parking availability for both GreenDay and Uni Wroc.

    Chart Overview#

    The chart plots free parking spaces over time. Two lines are drawn:

    LineParking lot
    First colour (default: green)GreenDay
    Second colour (default: blue)Uni Wroc

    Interacting with the Chart#

    Zooming#

    • Scroll (or pinch on touch devices) to zoom in/out on the time axis.
    • Use the zoom slider at the bottom of the chart to pan and zoom.
    • Zoom state is preserved when you switch colour palettes.

    Tooltip#

    • Hover (or tap) over the chart to see exact values for both parking lots at a given time.

    Colour Palette Selector#

    The Palette selector in the header (visible on wider screens) lets you choose from four built-in colour schemes:

    PaletteGreenDay colourUni Wroc colour
    Neon (default)🟢 Neon green🔵 Cyan blue
    Classic🔵 Blue🟡 Amber
    Cyberpunk💠 Cyan🟣 Magenta
    Modern🟢 Emerald🔵 Indigo

    Color Vision Mode override: When a non-normal color vision mode is selected in Settings, the chart automatically switches to a CB-aware palette regardless of the palette selector. The CB palette is derived from the active color-blindness mode and current theme (light/dark).

    Event Crossings#

    The chart displays vertical lines that mark specific moments; each colour has a defined meaning:

    • Orange: time when GreenDay (GD) free spaces reached zero.
    • Red: time when both GreenDay and Uni have no free places (total free = 0).
    • Blue: time when the first places free up in GreenDay — a “risky” reopening where freed places may be quickly taken.
    • Green: time when sufficient free capacity expected to remain available.

    These event lines correspond to the prediction types surfaced in the Predictions panel. The app shows four prediction tiles that summarize upcoming events:

    • GD=0 — predicted time when GreenDay is expected to reach zero free spaces.
    • All=0 — predicted time when total free spaces (both locations) are expected to reach zero.
    • Try FREE — predicted time when a small number of places are expected to become available.
    • Safe FREE — predicted time when a more stable amount of free capacity is expected.

    Each prediction tile displays the event time formatted as hh:mm. When a predicted time is within two hours, a live minute-countdown is shown on the tile; the countdown updates in real time. The countdown window is two hours by default. The times shown come from the model’s median estimate.

    Predictions header#

    The Predictions panel heading now displays the full weekday name. For example: Predictions for Monday.

    Symbols Toggle#

    Click the Symbols button to show or hide data point markers on the chart lines.
