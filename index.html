SYI Projects

Simple Yet Important utilities of PioterekS 🔧

Below are the projects served on https://piotereks.top.

  • Free Parking (free-parking) — An app that aggregates and visualizes corporate parking availability in Wrocław (Green Day and University leased lots) — real-time and historical. This was created to fill the gap in the original app that shows numbers without telling you if they’re up to date. It also helps sustainability: with a better overview you can plan trips and avoid unnecessary circulation. You can view the Dashboard and a Chart with 2 days of history.
    Run: /free-parking/

  • WordRef Search (esp-dict) — A Spanish–Spanish dictionary aggregator: type a word and get definitions, synonyms, the RAE definition, and an English translation. If you type an English word it will return the Spanish definition!!!
    Run: /esp-dict/

  • VCF Contact Generator (vcf-gen) — App to generate VCF contact files from 1 or 2 wildcards. The idea: create a contact file with many numbers and import it to your phone to help manage unwanted calls. Modern marketing, heh.
    Run: /vcf-gen/

  • Genealogia Sakowscy (genealogy) — Sakowski family tree and heatmap visualizations with a few embedded genealogy viewers.
    Run: /genealogy/
