    Overview#

    Free Parking Monitor is a real-time web application that shows the current number of free parking spaces at two locations in Wrocław, Poland:

    • GreenDay – up to 187 spaces
    • Uni Wroc (University of Wrocław) – up to 41 spaces

    The app refreshes automatically every 5 minutes and stores the last known values in your browser so you can see parking availability even if you are offline briefly.
